# Public Boundary

## Public Layer

This repository may contain:

- definitions
- conceptual frameworks
- historical teaching cases
- sanitized examples
- generic templates
- high-level diagrams

## Never Public By Default

The following are not public by default:

- internal scoring
- weights
- thresholds
- real industry assessments
- customer or business data
- internal prompts
- raw failure records
- correction rationale
- validation datasets
- production SOP
- resource-allocation logic
- internal decision rules

Public repositories are educational and conceptual representations, not complete replicas of the internal operating systems.
