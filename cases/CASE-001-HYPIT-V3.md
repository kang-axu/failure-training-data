## 1. Background

Hypit V3 was produced through an AI-assisted content-production workflow.

The workflow successfully reached a technically finished video.

However, the finished video did not pass Kang's final editorial quality review and was not published.

**Technically completed.**

**Editorially rejected.**

This makes Hypit V3 the first real Failure Training Data case in this repository.

The purpose of this case is not to prove that the current FTD specification is correct.

The purpose is to test whether FTD can preserve a real failure, reconstruct the human correction, validate later changes, and eventually alter future production behavior.

## 13. Why Publish an Incomplete Failure?

FTD is not intended to become a collection of polished success stories written after the fact.

The historical record should preserve the real order in which organizational experience was created:

**FAILURE → UNDERSTANDING → CORRECTION → VALIDATION → BEHAVIORAL UPDATE**

At the time this case was opened, only the failure and the human rejection were confirmed.

That incompleteness is part of the record.
